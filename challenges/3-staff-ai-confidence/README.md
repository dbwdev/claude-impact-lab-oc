# Challenge 3: Make AI approachable

> How might we help IHH staff discover what AI can do for their own work, and feel safe trying it
> again?

**Who it's for.** One frontline role, designed for a real person in it. IHH's first priority is
LVNs and medical assistants.

**Today.** AI tools are already close at hand, but staff aren't using them, and earlier trainings
didn't stick. IHH named why: AI feels abstract for their job, privacy worries, habits that are hard
to change on a busy shift, and nervousness about their jobs.

**If you crack it.** The gap isn't access to AI. It's the moment a staff member sees it do something
useful for their own work, and feels safe trying it.

## The brief

These are a direction, not a spec. Build what you think will help.

- **Build:** around one weekly task, not around AI. Useful within minutes, on a phone, in about 15
  minutes. Made-up clients only, clear about what's OK to paste.
- **A great demo:** a skeptical staff member finishes it without help, says "I'd use that for ___",
  and leaves with something to try on their next shift.
- **Out of scope:** a production scribe, connecting to IHH's record system, real client data,
  writing IHH policy. They stay in charge, and IHH can update it without a developer.

you try this on a busy day? Who on the team do others copy?
