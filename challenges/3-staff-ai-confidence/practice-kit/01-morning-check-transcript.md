# Morning check: what was said

**Made up for practice. Calvin Mercer and Tasha are not real people.**

Setting: a recuperative care site, Wednesday, 8:10 AM. Tasha is an LVN. Calvin Mercer, 58, is on
day 9 of his stay. He has type 2 diabetes and a healing ulcer on his left heel.

---

**Tasha:** Morning, Mr. Mercer. How did you sleep?

**Calvin:** Okay. My heel was throbbing around two. Woke me up.

**Tasha:** How bad, zero to ten?

**Calvin:** About a four then. It's more like a two now.

**Tasha:** Okay. Let me get your vitals. Blood pressure is 138 over 84. Pulse 78. Temperature 98.4.
Oxygen 97. Go ahead and check your sugar for me.

**Calvin:** *(checks with his own meter)* One eighty-six.

**Tasha:** Before breakfast. What was last night's?

**Calvin:** Two twelve before dinner.

**Tasha:** Did you take your insulin last night?

**Calvin:** No. I fell asleep early and missed it.

**Tasha:** The 24 units?

**Calvin:** Yeah. I took my metformin this morning though. *(shows the pill organizer and takes the
morning dose while she watches)*

**Tasha:** Thank you. How are you on test strips?

**Calvin:** Maybe four left.

**Tasha:** I'll flag that. Let me look at the foot. The dressing is dry and still sealed. No
drainage coming through, no smell. I don't see any redness above the ankle. The home health nurse
is coming today at one to change it.

**Calvin:** Okay.

**Tasha:** Your clinic appointment is Thursday at 10:30. Do you have a ride?

**Calvin:** No.

**Tasha:** I'll ask the case manager to book one. Anything else on your mind?

**Calvin:** I keep thinking about where I go after this.

**Tasha:** That's a fair thing to worry about. Your housing assessment with the case manager is
Friday. I'll make sure she knows it's weighing on you.

**Calvin:** Thanks.

**Tasha:** So here's what I'm doing. I'm telling the case manager about the ride and the strips.
I'm writing down the missed dose so the doctor sees it Thursday. I'm not changing anything with
your insulin. That's for the doctor.
