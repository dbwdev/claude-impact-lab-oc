# Your first hackathon

What to expect, in two minutes.

## You do not need

- **A team.** Teams form at kickoff. Come alone; most people do.
- **An idea.** IHH brings the problems. You pick one with your team.
- **To write code.** Claude writes code from plain descriptions. Teams also need people who
  understand the work, test the tool, write the words on screen and run the demo.
- **Anything prepared.** Nothing built before 9:30 AM counts, and no pre-work earns credit.

## What the day looks like

Kickoff is about half an hour. Then you build for most of the day, with lunch at your table.
At 4:30 PM you hand in what you have, and judges come to every table to see it working.
A few finalists then show their work to the room.

## What usually works

- **Small and working beats big and unfinished.** One person, one task, done well.
- **Ask early.** Show a rough version to an IHH staff member before lunch. Change it based on what
  they say.
- **Decide what you are building in the first hour.** Then build.
- **Save often.** If you are using GitHub, push your work every hour or so.
- **Keep the demo simple.** Show the thing working on one realistic example.

## Normal things that happen

- Your first idea changes. That is fine.
- Something breaks an hour before the deadline. Hand in what works and say what does not.
- A team is not working for you in the first hour. Talk to an organizer; switching early is fine.
- You get stuck on setup. Ask an organizer. Nobody should lose the morning to an install.
