## Illumination Foundation: Mission Overview

- Serves 1,000+ people experiencing homelessness; 2,500+ through recuperative care annually
- Three core service areas:
  - Family emergency homes (addressing intergenerational homelessness and adverse childhood experiences)
  - Recuperative care for medically frail individuals leaving hospital
  - Senior microcommunity housing (fastest-growing homeless demographic)
- Cost savings case: recuperative care saves ~$8,000 in Medicaid costs per member per year
  - ~$20M in total savings based on 2,500 people served last year (per 2021 white paper)

## Problem 1: Referral Processing (Recuperative Care)

- Hospital case managers send referral packets, sometimes hundreds of pages, as PDFs
- Referral team must review each page against admissions criteria and insurance authorization requirements
- Slow processing causes patients to stay in hospital longer, or leave against medical advice
- Faster and more accurate review = better patient outcomes and lower system costs
- Goal: AI tool to process referrals faster and reduce missed information

## Problem 2: Client Resource Navigation

- Case managers have high caseloads and limited time per client
- Clients have varying levels of literacy and technology literacy
- Goal: AI tool to help clients independently find and navigate community resources
  - Builds independent skills, reduces reliance on case managers

## Problem 3: AI Literacy for Staff

- Staff unfamiliarity with AI drives fear that it will replace jobs
- Goal: build an introductory AI curriculum for staff
  - Help staff understand how AI works
  - Build comfort and confidence using AI in their roles

## Sean Baker: Impact Story

- Lived on the streets of Orange County for 10 years with a congenital heart defect (5 open-heart surgeries)
- Discharged to Illumination Foundation’s recuperative care after surgery
- Partnership between Illumination Foundation, OC Health Care Agency, and CalOptima Health (via CalAIM)
  - CalAIM funds services addressing complex social needs, including housing
  - OC Housing Authority provided rental vouchers; Illumination Foundation handled client support and landlord negotiation
- Now lives in a 1-bedroom apartment; heart health described by his doctor as “normal” for the first time
- 100+ people with chronic illnesses now housed in Orange County through this partnership
